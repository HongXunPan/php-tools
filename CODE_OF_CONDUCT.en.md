# Code of Conduct

We want `hongxunpan/php-tools` to be a professional, welcoming, inclusive, and evidence-oriented open-source community. By participating in this repository, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This policy applies to Issues, Pull Requests, code reviews, discussions, and other public or private communication in which a participant represents the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Respect differences in experience, background, viewpoint, and communication style;
- Focus on the problem, evidence, and verifiable results rather than attacking individuals;
- State assumptions, impact, and uncertainty clearly;
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes promptly;
- Protect privacy, security reports, and unpublished sensitive information;
- Remain patient during disagreements and prefer outcomes that support the project's long-term health.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats, or personal attacks;
- Publishing another person's private information, credentials, or security-report details;
- Intentionally posting spam, malicious code, or misleading information;
- Repeatedly disrupting discussion, refusing to follow maintenance processes, or deliberately consuming maintainer resources;
- Other conduct that would clearly be inappropriate in a professional collaboration environment.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this policy. Depending on the nature and impact of a violation, they may:

1. provide a private reminder and request corrective action;
2. issue a public warning or restrict participation;
3. temporarily prohibit interaction;
4. permanently prohibit participation.

Reports will be handled with appropriate confidentiality, and unnecessary sensitive details will not be distributed.

## Reporting

To report a Code of Conduct incident, email `me@kangxuanpeng.com` with relevant links, dates, context, and the outcome you are requesting.

Do not use a public Issue for incidents involving privacy, personal safety, or unpublished security information. Good-faith reporters will not face retaliation for raising a concern.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers should process reports consistently and fairly, avoid conflicts of interest, and share information only as necessary to resolve the issue. Reports that are clearly unsupported, malicious, or abusive may be closed without further action.

## Reference

This policy draws on the community commitments, scope, and enforcement principles of [Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/) and adapts them to the project's current single-maintainer governance model.
